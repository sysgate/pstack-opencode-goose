The question: for each of six signals Jev gives a probability that a tool call is risky. The gate escalates (asks you) when any signal reaches its threshold. Lower thresholds catch more dangerous commands but ask you more often. Where should each threshold sit, and does it stay inside the bar? The bar: every dangerous command escalated, no more than about 5% of your real routine commands escalated, at most 10 extra prompts on a working day.
