## What contradicts an accepted decision

**1. The "Reopened" node and its edge ("contradiction → reopen").** This is the one real contradiction. The edge runs from Closure to a node labelled "Reopened · 修界 fail-closed".
- Doctrine now says `CLOSED` is terminal: reopening a hyperedge or semantic object starts a **new `CANDIDATE`** that names the earlier one in its `predecessor_ids`; the earlier one is never resumed (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §7, `AGENTS.md` Revision Agent, ADR-level decision L2).
- "Reopened" also is not a state (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 has no such state), and a contradiction after closure is not a "fail-closed" outcome of 修界: nothing failed, a new candidate begins.
- The wording came from the white paper's old `Closed → Reopened only by formal trigger` line, which I already removed from the paper.
- Where the string appears: `Reopened` at lines 5102, 5103, 5110; `reopen` at 4950, 5132, 5134; `fail-closed` (the sub-label) at 5102, 5103, 5111. The internal id `closure_stop` appears at 4950, 5102 and 5132 and can stay as it is.
